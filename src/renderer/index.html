Click a rune to finish a quest. Double-click to edit. Drag a board by its title. Right-click a quest to remove it. T = theme, M = sound.

Backups

Every change to your quests is saved, on this device and on the devbox. Pick a moment to bring that board back; your current board is backed up first, so restoring can be undone too.

    Sync your board

    Point every device you own at your own sync server and they share one board. Without sync the board stays on this device. Setting up a server takes a few minutes; the guide (and a prompt you can hand to your AI agent) is at github.com/Mild-Solvent/Questboard/blob/main/docs/SYNC.md. Never use someone else's address and token: you would be editing their board.
